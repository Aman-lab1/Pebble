# 🎨 Design Philosophy

Pebble follows a minimalist, iOS-inspired design language focused on speed, clarity, and distraction-free interactions.

Every feature is designed around one question:

> Can this help users understand or record their spending with fewer taps and less friction?

If the answer is no, it doesn't belong in Pebble.

# 📲 Install Pebble

Pebble is a Progressive Web App (PWA), so there's nothing to download from an app store.

### 🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open Pebble in **Safari**:

   [https://aman-lab1.github.io/Pebble/](https://aman-lab1.github.io/Pebble/)

2. Tap the **Share** button (⬆️).

3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

4. Tap **Add**.

Pebble will now appear on your Home Screen and launch like a native app.

---

### 🤖 Android (Chrome)

1. Open Pebble in **Google Chrome**:

   [https://aman-lab1.github.io/Pebble/](https://aman-lab1.github.io/Pebble/)

2. Tap the **⋮** (three-dot menu).

3. Tap **Install**.

Pebble will now launch as a standalone app from your Home Screen.
